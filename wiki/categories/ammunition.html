Ammunition

Article

Ammunition adds damage to bow or magic attacks. Compare each family in progression order below.

Arrows#

Equip arrows for bow attacks. Each tier adds the damage bonus shown below. The table follows progression from the first tier to the last.

Use the Crafting Table and choose Arrows. Materials can come from your Bag or Bank. Each recipe uses the quantities listed to make its output; no crafting skill level is required.

Spellstones#

Equip spellstones for magic attacks. Bind them at the Spellstone Pool. Every binding uses 10 × Meteor Shard and, for colored tiers, one catalyst from that row. Materials can come from your Bag or Bank; no skill level is required.

Right-click Meteor Core and choose Break Down to get 10 × Meteor Shard.

Fusing spellstones#

At the same pool, fuse 2 spellstones of the immediately preceding tier into 1 of the next tier. Fusion needs no shards or catalyst and awards no XP.
