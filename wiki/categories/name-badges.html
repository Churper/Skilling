Name Badges

Article

Name Badges colour your name and add their icon beside it. Add a badge from your Bag to your Kit, then select it for the Badge slot.

Appearance and unlock requirements#

Skill badges unlock at level 300 in the named skill. Each preview shows the requirement for that style. Event badges use participation counts; podium badges use podium points. The Lv beside the example player name is combat level.
