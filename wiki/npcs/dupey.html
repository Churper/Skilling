Mythic upgrades#

Dupey consumes offerings for a chance to upgrade a Legendary item to Mythic. Choose an unbroken Legendary equipment item or wisp in your Bag or equipment slots. Dragon Tusk Amulet cannot be a target or an offering. Offerings are destroyed even when the upgrade fails.

Add the contributions from all items offered, then apply the cap. Wisp targets receive twice the equipment chance. Extra offerings beyond the cap do not improve the attempt. Values below are percentage points added per item, before that cap.

Complete accepted offerings#

Put offerings in your Bag. Locked, enchantment-locked, broken and bound items cannot be offered, and equipped items must be moved to the Bag first. Gems must be ordinary loose gems; collectible offerings must be their ordinary version. Only the seven boss equipment items listed below accept rarity variants. Mythic items are not offerings. The chosen target is reserved and cannot also be fed, although spare copies in its stack can be used.
