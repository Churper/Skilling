Party Hall

Article

Enter the Party Hall to host or join a drop party. Players donate prizes, the host releases balloons, and visitors pop them to claim rewards. The mainland map's Party Room marker points to this entrance.

What to click inside#

Party Hall interactions
Deposit ChestSign in, choose coins or items from your Bank, then confirm a donation to the current open party. Each deposit supports up to 8 different item stacks. You choose quantities before submitting.
Host ControlsStart a party, announce it to other players, release the balloons or end your hosted session.
BalloonsPop balloons during the drop to claim their prizes. Item rewards are delivered to your Bank; coin rewards go to your balance.
Claim ReturnsWhen returned goods are waiting, use the explicit Claim Returns action to reclaim them. They are not restored by simply reopening the chest.
Leave Party HallUse the exit to return to the mainland entrance.
