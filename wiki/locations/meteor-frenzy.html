Meteor Frenzy is a Frenzy event that can trigger while mining at an eligible node.

The game checks for an offer every 10 seconds, with a 8% base chance per check. A claimed offer awards 2–7 Frenzy items from the tier matching your Mining level. While charging a Mystic Orb, the Frenzy item reward is skipped.
