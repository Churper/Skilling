Lure Fire burns logs over time to earn Critter Fur and progress toward Box Trap Latches.

Choose an eligible log and feed the fire. See Hunting Lodge fuel for fuel requirements and rewards.

What each log gives#

Per log burned. Storm Latch and up (Lvl 600+) get more progress. Latch chances are on the Latches page.
