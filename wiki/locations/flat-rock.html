Flat Rock

Article

The Flat Rock beside Slimmy is the feeding spot for his crocodile quest. It is an interactable, not a mining rock.

Feeding the crocodile#

Talk to Slimmy first. Carry a supported meat cut in your Bag and click the rock to feed it. You need 5 feeds before following the sleepy crocodile downriver. If you carry several eligible cuts, the dialogue lets you choose which one to use.

Eligible crocodile food
Prime Cut
Ribeye
Tenderloin
Seared Prime Cut
Cooked Ribeye
Cooked Tenderloin

What happens next#

Stay close to the crocodile as it travels. Investigate the blocked cave, return to Slimmy and speak to Cap'n Bubbles about getting inside. The quest's later material request is 100 × Coconut Palm Logs, 100 × Moss Stone, 100 × Spore Crystal. Continue from the quest instructions to receive Shovel and explore the Croc Den.

The cave entrance is revealed through this quest. It is not a permanent cave that every new account can enter immediately.
