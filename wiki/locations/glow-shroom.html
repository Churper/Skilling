Glow Shroom is a manually plucked mushroom inside island caves.

Plucking Glow Shrooms#

Click a Glow Shroom node inside an island cave. This is a manual pluck, rather than a timed AFK gather. A successful node is depleted for that cave’s daily run.

Higher Survival levels do not multiply the number plucked. Each Glow Shroom occupies a bag slot.

Feeding the Stone Slime ring#

Carry Glow Shroom in your bag and click an uncharged Stone Slime in the cave shrine’s ring. Each stone consumes 1 Glow Shroom. A ring contains 6–9 stones, depending on the cave. Charged stones light up and the cave records your progress.

Charge every stone to awaken the Giant Cave Slime. Feeding an already charged stone does not spend another mushroom.

Location#

Enter an island cave and find its shrine and mushrooms inside. See islands and sailing. Cave interiors have their own space and are not plotted as fixed overworld coordinates.
