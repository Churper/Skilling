Bush Patch

Location

Type

Mapped places
3
View on map

Bush Patch grows a personal bush that you can pick when mature.

Choose a bush, meet its Survival and Farming requirements, then pay for planting. The grown bush has a limited stock of resources. Remove it or gather it down before planting another. See Farming.
