Crop Patch is a farming patch.

Choose a crop and compost tier, pay its planting cost, then return when it is ready to harvest. A crop requires its listed Farming level. Higher Farming levels improve the harvest, while compost changes yield, growth time and other benefits. See Farming and compost.

The yield table lists each crop’s base range at its unlock level. Each crop’s item article includes a harvest calculator for your Farming level, compost and crop-yield Wisp bonus. Level bonus crops and Wisp bonus crops do not grant extra crop XP.
