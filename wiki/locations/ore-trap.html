Ore Trap uses ore over time to earn Critter Fur and progress toward Box Trap Latches.

Choose an eligible ore and fuel the trap. See Hunting Lodge fuel for fuel requirements and rewards.

What each ore gives#

Per ore burned. Storm Latch and up (Lvl 600+) get more progress. Latch chances are on the Latches page.
