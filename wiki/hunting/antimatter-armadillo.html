Antimatter Armadillo is a Hunting target requiring Hunting 2000. Catch it at a Antimatter Armadillo Hunting Spot.

Click the animal to start catching with your Box Trap. Each successful catch uses one berry bowl from your bag and gives 1 Antimatter Essence. Upgrade your trap with Trappy; see tool upgrades.
