Fish Frenzy rewards

Article

Your Fishing level decides the bonus fish

Click the purple fishing spot during its frenzy window. The frenzy catch awards 10× XP and a bonus fish from the table below. Deposit that fish at the Washing Machine for charges, or keep it for other uses.

Fish Frenzy rewards by Fishing level
Fishing levelRewardWasher charges each
1–99Minnow1
100–199Trophy Cowfish2
200–299Marlin3
300–399Leviathan4
400+Kraken5

The Kraken fish is a frenzy reward, separate from the Kraken boss encounter.
