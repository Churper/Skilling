The Ville

Article

The Ville is Slimeville's neighborhood town. Buy and upgrade your home, decorate it, visit other houses and explore its public places.

Getting to the Ville#

Use the mainland Enter the Ville sign. A charged Home Stone can also take you directly to the Ville.

Town and homes#

Open Plots to browse neighborhoods and available homes. Use My home to locate your property. A home is bought with Cozy Coupon, then improved one stage at a time. See Player housing for prices, decorating and working facilities.

Walk around town or use its taxi to visit Town square, Farming Guild, Woodcutters’ Grove, Pebble Mine, Shell Beach. Speak to the Ville squirrels for Home Stone purchases and recharging.

Home Stone travel#

Feed a Ville Squirrel 100 Legendary Jelly to buy Home Stone with 10 charges. The accepted jellies are the dishes in the Legendary Jelly recipes.

Carry the stone in your Bag and choose My House or The Ville from its menu. Each teleport uses one charge; travelling to your house requires owning a home. Left-click can ask each time or remember either destination.

The stone works from the mainland, the Ville, a player house or the Peaceful Meadow. Its destinations are the Ville and your own house. Other instances block the trip. Moving, jumping, fighting or gathering cancels the cast before a charge is spent.

Bring the stone and Legendary Jelly in your Bag back to a Ville Squirrel to recharge: 3 Jelly per charge, up to 100 charges. If a paid trip fails to open its destination, use the offered retry to finish that trip without paying another charge.
