Pet Garden

Article
On this pageYour slimeletsWork & careScroll perksScroll training

Raise slimelets in the Pet Garden, train them to gather supplies, and equip one as your companion to use scroll perks. Talk to Farmer Sprout to manage the Garden.

Your slimelets#

The Garden holds up to 12 slimelets. Your first egg is free; later eggs cost SlimeCoins and become more expensive as your roster grows. Body shape and colour change appearance. Nature affects training: a matching nature doubles XP in its favoured stat.

Work, food and care#

Slimelets train in woodcutting, mining, fishing, scribing, farming, faith, survival. Assigned workers need Tree Meat in the shared trough. Each Tree Meat fuels one working slimelet for 24 minutes; more workers consume fuel faster. Happiness controls gathering effectiveness, with a 25% floor. Hunger is not a separate care requirement.

Each Pet Treat permanently adds 5% gathering rate, up to 20 treats per slimelet. Each treat also raises its care floor by 5 points. A fully treated slimelet still needs trough fuel to work.

Magic Seeds and the Magic Blossom#

Plant Magic Seed in the Garden’s Magic Patch to activate Magic Blossom: +10% pet gathering XP for 10 hours per seed. Planting more seeds extends the remaining time. Workers still consume Tree Meat from the trough. Clicking a Magic Seed in your bag also lets you change to another owned companion for 1 seed; changing at the Garden gate and dismissing a companion are free.

Boss scrolls and companion perks#

Feed boss scrolls from your bag to any owned slimelet. Each scroll is consumed and adds the charges below, plus Power to the slimelet you feed. Charges form one shared account pool. Your equipped companion's Power sets the chance of saving a charge when a perk is used. The 13 perk types are listed below; Bone Scroll is an alternative source of the same Alch charges as Skele Scroll.

Perks require an active companion and available charges. Key names are the defaults and can be rebound. Auto-loot and Auto-eat can be paused. Alch is used through a bag item’s right-click menu.

Resource scroll training#

Resource scrolls train the slimelet, rather than giving your character XP directly. Base XP scales from 1× at slimelet stat level 1 to 20× at level 500; a matching nature doubles the result. Your active companion also trains from your own skill XP, starting at 0.125% before Wisp and nature bonuses.
