Tempo

Article

Tempo makes your combat attacks faster as you keep landing hits. It has two parts: your total Tempo from worn gear and buffs, and Tempo stacks built during combat.

On this pageTempo and stacksBuilding and keeping stacksWhere Tempo comes fromExamples

Tempo and stacks#

A Tempo line is a potential on equipment. A line showing +3 Tempo adds 3 to your total Tempo. Two such lines give 6 Tempo. Add the values on all your worn gear together.

A Tempo stack is temporary combat progress. Each qualifying hit adds one stack, up to 18 normally. More total Tempo makes those stacks give a stronger attack speed bonus. Adding a line increases your Tempo value; landing another hit increases your stack counter.

Building and keeping stacks#

  • Have a Tempo source active, then land hits to build stacks. Melee, bow and magic all use Tempo.
  • Attacks do not spend stacks. Keep hitting to reach the cap and maintain it.
  • A miss adds no stack. Existing stacks remain until more than 3.5 seconds pass without a qualifying hit, then they reset together.
  • Changing targets does not clear the counter by itself. Keep the gap between hits short.
  • Stacks are kept through the protected phase gaps for a flying Dragon, an untargetable Snake Boss and a frozen Frost Yeti while that fight remains your active target.

Equipping Skele Ring raises the cap by 10, from 18 to 28 stacks. The ring supplies extra capacity; you still need Tempo from another source to start building stacks.

Where Tempo comes from#

Equipment potentials: roll Tempo at the Washing Machine. A normal line gives +1 to +3 Tempo; a Jackpot can reach +4. Potential values on worn equipment add together. See gear and upgrades for the other ways to improve equipment.

Tempo Potion: adds +3 Tempo for 5 minutes per potion, on top of your worn gear. It can start Tempo stacking even with no Tempo lines. Drinking more extends its time at the same strength. It adds Tempo points to the speed curve; it does not immediately grant stacks or increase the stack cap.

Lynx Wisp: attach it to worn equipment to add Tempo alongside your potential lines. Its rarity changes the amount; see the Wisp's item page for each value.

For example, a single +3 Tempo line at 18 stacks gives about +28.97% attack speed. Two such lines give about +47.53% at the same stack count. More lines keep helping, with a smooth curve rather than a fixed percentage for each stack.

Tempo adds its attack speed bonus alongside effects such as Swiftness Potion. Weapon tier also affects the underlying attack interval. The final speed you see depends on the whole loadout.

How the Tempo speed bonus is calculated

Let T be total Tempo and S be your stacks. The speed ceiling is 57 + min(1.27 × T, 110) + 0.8 × √S. The attack speed bonus is that ceiling × (1 − exp(−T × S × 0.8 ÷ 72)) percent. With zero Tempo or zero stacks, the bonus is zero.
