Dupey

Article
On this pageMaking an attemptSuccess chance limitsAccepted offerings

Dupey upgrades a selected Legendary equipment item or Legendary Wisp to Mythic. Failed attempts keep the selected item; all offered items are consumed.

Making an attempt#

  1. Select a Legendary from your Bag or equipped gear.
  2. Add eligible spare offerings from your Bag and check the displayed success chance.
  3. Confirm the attempt. A success upgrades the selected item; a failure leaves it Legendary.

Locked, enchanted-locked, broken and bound items cannot be offerings. Mythic equipment has 2.25× base stats. Open Wisps for Wisp bonuses.
