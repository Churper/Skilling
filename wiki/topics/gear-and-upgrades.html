Gear & upgrades

Article
🛡️

Craft gear at the Crafting Table using Ore and Logs. Equip across 9 worn slots: Body, Cape, 2 Rings, Amulet, Shield, Sword, Bow, Staff.

Monsters also drop equipment! Higher-tier monsters = better gear drops.
