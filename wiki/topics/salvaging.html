Salvaging is a boat activity that brings up sunken bundles, lost purses and rare treasures while you rest on the ocean. Fit a Salvaging Net, stop your boat and lower the hook.

Getting started and locations#

  1. Visit the Port, open the boat shop and choose Add-ons.
  2. Buy the Salvaging Net using the materials below. It stays fitted on every boat you own.
  3. Launch your boat and come to a complete stop on the ocean. Salvaging has no special island or fishing spot requirement.
  4. Click the Salvaging Hook control to lower the hook. Stop other gathering first.

Salvaging has a 6-hour AFK timer. Clicks, key presses, touch and scroll input refill the allowance while salvaging is still active. Just switching back to the tab does not refill it. Once the timer expires, lower the hook again to start another 6-hour allowance.

A background or minimised tab can keep salvaging until that deadline. If you close the game with the hook down, returning collects elapsed finds only up to the saved deadline; closing does not reset or extend the timer. Moving your boat, leaving it or clicking the hook to reel in stops salvaging.

Nets and hooks#

Buy each upgrade in order. The listed materials buy that rank, and previous ranks stay paid for.

The Salvaging Net finds one item every 30 seconds. Each hook upgrade removes 1 second from that interval. Hook upgrades change the number of finds, while the chance of each item per find stays the same. A running session keeps the interval it started with; reel in and lower the hook again after upgrading.

With an upgraded hook, multiply the net hourly averages by 30 ÷ your seconds per find. For example, Antimatter Hook gives 1.875 times as many finds on average.

Leviathan Wisp bonuses on worn equipment can add one independent extra loot roll per find, using the same drop table. The combined bonus is capped at a 100% extra-roll chance. Extra rolls give loot without extra XP.

Opening finds#

Open Waterlogged Log, Sea Rock, Tangled Net and Lost Purse from your Bag. The resource pool uses your matching skill level when you open the bundle, so saving bundles for a later level changes what they can contain. Opening gives the contents and consumes the bundle.

One bundle selects one resource type. There is an 80% chance to choose uniformly from your 5 most recently unlocked resources, and a 20% chance to choose uniformly from every unlocked resource in that skill. If fewer than 5 are unlocked, both rolls use the smaller pool. A selected resource whose own requirement is 600 or higher gives 1; other resources give 2–5.

For an exact resource chance, let N be all your unlocked resources in the skill and M the smaller of 5 and N. A resource among your M newest unlocks has 0.8 ÷ M + 0.2 ÷ N chance per bundle; an older one has 0.2 ÷ N.

Opening one bundle sends its materials to your Bag, with Bank fallback when needed. The Bag menu's batch opening sends materials to the Bank.

Lost Purses#

Each Lost Purse gives Gold and rolls a Slimecoin amount, with an independent extra Ancient Coin roll. Gold scales with your Explorer level when the purse is opened.

Experience#

Every find awards Explorer XP. A Waterlogged Log also awards Woodcutting XP, a Sea Rock awards Mining XP, and a Tangled Net awards Fishing XP. XP comes from finding the bundle, not opening it.

Resource skill XP scales with the best ordinary gathering available at your level and the best unlocked ordinary tool. At the basic net's pace, each skill averages about 10% of that unboosted gathering benchmark. Equipment, potions, events and PvP bonuses are excluded from the benchmark. Faster hooks produce more finds and therefore more XP. Explorer XP per find scales with your level.
