The Washing Machine rolls three potential stat lines on equipment. Deposit frenzy items to gain washer charges, then spend those charges to roll your worn gear. New accounts start with 10 charges.

Getting washer charges#

Deposit accepted drops from your bag, bank or Frenzy Bag. Items are consumed when deposited. Each has a fixed charge value: for example, Gold Nugget supplies 2 charges and Meteor Core supplies 3.

Washing your gear#

  1. Equip your item. Select its worn slot at the Washing Machine and check the item name.
  2. Roll the potential. Every unlocked line is replaced. Lock up to two lines to keep them.
  3. Set an auto-roll target if needed. Choose a stat and a minimum value. Auto-roll stops when an unlocked line matches, you press Stop, or your charges run out. A matching locked line does not count.

Its article explains how Tempo lines, stacks and the cap work. Luck helps frenzy frequency and washer roll quality. Locks preserve the exact values you already have.

Transpose#

Transpose moves all potential lines from an old item onto a new one. Choose the source and destination carefully: success removes the source’s lines and replaces the destination’s lines. A failed attempt keeps both items’ lines, but still spends charges. The panel shows the target item’s charge cost. The destination item’s wiki page also lists its Transpose cost.

What an improved transfer does#

On the 2% improved outcome, each source line gets a separate value roll from 110% up to 125% of that stat’s normal maximum, rounded to the nearest whole percentage point. The transfer keeps the higher of that roll and the existing source value. The improvement chance applies once to the whole attempt; each line then rolls its own value.

This rerolls against the stat’s maximum, rather than adding a percentage to your current line. There is no guaranteed increase: rounding or an already high line can leave it unchanged. Existing values above the normal or improved ceiling are preserved. XP Boost stays at 1% because its maximum is 1% and the improvement rounds back to the same value.
