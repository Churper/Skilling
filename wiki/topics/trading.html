Player Exchange

Cross-server marketplace for items & gear

The Exchange is the global trading marketplace. Registered, non-Ironslime players can post items for sale or place buy orders - listings appear globally and any player can fulfill them. Unlike the clan vault, the Exchange handles gear with SlimePower and potential lines, making it the right place to move enchanted equipment.

💡 Use the Exchange for gear (it preserves SlimePower + potential lines) and bulk resource trades. Use the clan Vault for shared stockpiles within your clan.

Direct trades & mail

For a direct trade, open another player’s interaction menu and choose Trade. Review both item offers and coin amounts before accepting. Changing an offer means it needs to be accepted again.

Open the Mailbox to read messages and claim available attachments or Exchange payouts. Check the recipient and contents before sending player mail. Ironslime accounts cannot use player trades or mail.

You do not need to join a clan to use the Exchange. Open the Player Exchange service to browse offers, post a listing and claim completed trades. Ironslime accounts cannot use it.
