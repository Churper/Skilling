AFK gathering

Article
On this pageGathering windowsRefresh CharmsOpen, background and closed gameWhat resets the timer

Click a resource to start gathering. Your slime keeps gathering while you are away until its AFK timer or another gathering limit stops it.

For resource gathering, a worn Cloud Wisp extends the window by its rarity’s AFK timer bonus; multiple worn bonuses add together. This does not extend Salvaging. See Wisps.

Salvaging has its own 6 hours timer. Activity refills it while the hook is active; after it expires, lower the hook again. Closing the game preserves earned finds up to the saved deadline, but does not start a new window.

Refresh Charms#

Refresh Charm automatically buys another eligible gathering window when its timer is due. Keep charms in your bag or bank. Right-click a charm to enable or disable automatic use.

One purchase at the SlimeCoin Shop gives 3 charms for 10 SlimeCoins. A refresh uses the charge cost in the table. It extends the existing deadline by one window, rather than giving credit for an unlimited gap.

Open, background and closed game#

Gathering while away
Open and unattendedGathering continues until its timer or another limit stops it. Eligible Refresh Charms can buy further windows.
Open in a background or minimised windowThe same rules apply. When the game wakes, it reconciles progress and any eligible paid extensions.
Closed, reloaded or discardedNo new Refresh Charms are spent. Eligible resources use Welcome Back progress instead.

Refresh Charms do not prevent a node depleting, an event ending, your bag filling or gathering being interrupted. They cannot extend resources marked “Not eligible” in the table.

What resets the timer#

A click or tap, keypress or scroll counts as activity and renews the gathering timer. Passive mouse movement and bringing the game back into focus do not reset it. The game settles progress up to the existing deadline before applying a new interaction, so returning after expiry does not retroactively extend the old gather.
