SlimePower bonuses

Article

ATK and DEF by SP level and gear tier

SlimePower adds ATK and DEF to equipped gear. Higher SP levels and gear tiers give larger bonuses.

A weapon's ATK bonus applies when using its combat style. Rings, amulets and capes use their own tier; skill capes and most special capes use Cobalt.

Item rarity does not change SP bonuses.
