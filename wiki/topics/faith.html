A Sacred Grove is the temporary crop-circle event used to train Faith. Its target label and spawn announcement use Sacred Grove.

Channel, then explore#

  1. Find a Sacred Grove and stand inside its circle to channel.
  2. Charge for up to 3 minutes. More charge improves the orb tier. A full charge also gives bonus channel XP.
  3. When channeling finishes, use the 2-minute exploration window to walk and collect the Faith orbs that appear.

Walking out pauses channeling. The status message shows the time remaining to return; if you do not return, your accumulated channel is finalized.
