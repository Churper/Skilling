Washing Machine

Article

Deposit drops, choose worn gear, keep good lines and roll the rest

Washing puts three potential stat lines on an item. It spends washer charges, not coins. New accounts start with 10 charges.

  1. Deposit fuel. Open the Washing Machine and deposit frenzy drops from your bag, bank or Frenzy Bag. The deposit list shows the charges per item. Ordinary frenzy chains give 1–5 charges per item; Enchanted Relic gives 6 and Meteor Core gives 3. Deposited drops are consumed.
  2. Select gear. Equip the item first, then select its worn slot in the washer. Check the item name before spending charges.
  3. Roll and keep good lines. A roll replaces every unlocked line. Lock a line you want to keep; you can lock up to two. Each roll costs 1 charge with no locks, 4 with one lock, or 10 with two locks.
  4. Auto-roll toward a target. Choose a stat and minimum value, then press Auto-roll until match. It stops when an unlocked line matches, you press Stop, or charges run out. A matching locked line does not count, so you can keep one good line while searching for another.
Each auto-roll spends the normal charge cost for your locks. Set the target before starting and check your charge balance.

Jackpot rolls can exceed a line’s normal cap. Tempo normally rolls 1–3%; a Jackpot roll can reach 4%. XP Boost stays at 1%, including Jackpot rolls. The last column shows each line’s Jackpot maximum.

Tempo builds attack-speed stacks as you land hits, up to 18 stacks. Luck helps frenzy frequency and washer roll quality. Locks preserve the exact values you already have.

Keep good potentials when upgrading gear

The washer’s Transpose option moves all potential lines from an old item onto a new one. Choose the source and destination carefully: success removes the source’s lines and replaces the destination’s lines. A failed attempt keeps both items’ lines, but still spends charges. The panel shows the target item’s charge cost and the chances of a transfer or an improved transfer.
