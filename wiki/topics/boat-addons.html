Salvaging Net & Hook Upgrades

Article

Permanent add-ons for every boat you own

Open the boat shop's Add-ons tab to buy a Salvaging Net, then upgrade through the hooks in order. Each hook requires the Explorer level shown and makes salvage finds one second faster than the previous rank. These upgrades stay fitted when you change your boat's appearance.
