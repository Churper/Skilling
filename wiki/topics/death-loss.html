On this pageWhat you can lose

Ordinary PvE deaths and dangerous PvP deaths have different loss rules. Check which mode you are entering before risking possessions.

What you can lose#

Death rules
Ordinary PvE deathRespawn without losing items, coins or XP.
Peaceful Crucible entryPossessions are kept.
Dangerous Crucible / PvP entryItems and coins can be lost. The entry screen shows protected items, always-kept items and locks that break.
