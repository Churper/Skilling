Wisps

Article
On this pageEvery WispRarityCrafting a Wisp

Glowing energy from Hunting that adds a bonus line to your gear

  1. Catch animals. When an animal hops out of your trap, it leaves a little of its essence behind, like Rabbit Essence.
  2. Craft a Wisp from essence at the Wisp Well. Every craft rolls a rarity.
  3. Put the Wisp on an item. Click the Wisp in your Bag and pick a gear piece. It adds one bonus line to that item. Each item holds one Wisp: a new one sends the old one back to your Bag. Right-click the item and pick Remove Wisp to take it off. Rarity only changes the number, never the effect.
  4. Salvage the ones you don't want. Extra Wisps turn back into essence.

Rarity#

A Goo Wisp at each rarity, with the chance to roll it. Higher rarity means a brighter Wisp and a larger bonus. Goo gives 0.25%, 0.5%, 0.75%, 1%, 1.25%, and 1.5% XP from Common to Mythic. Equipped Goo Wisps add together, up to 13.5% with nine Mythic Wisps. Mythic comes from Dupey; it cannot be crafted or refined at the Wisp Well.

Crafting a Wisp#

At the Wisp Well, from that animal's essence

Each craft makes one Wisp at the rarity odds shown above. Crafting needs the animal's Hunting level. Salvaging a Wisp of any rarity returns 50% of its base crafting essence, rounded down. Essence spent refining it is not refunded.

Refining raises a Wisp you own one rarity for more of its essence: Uncommon costs 1.5 crafts, Rare 2.5, Epic 5 and Legendary 12.5. Taking a Common all the way to Legendary costs 21.5 crafts of essence.

Catch time is how long one craft's essence takes with the best box trap at that Hunting level, misses included.

Essence prices target the average gold per hour of ordinary Woodcutting and Mining at the same level, after paying for a plain Berry Bowl per catch. Wisp prices preserve that expected income across the crafting rarity odds. The table shows Common Wisp prices; higher rarities sell for more.
