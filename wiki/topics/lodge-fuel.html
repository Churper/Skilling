Lure Fire & Ore Trap

Article

The Lure Fire burns logs and the Ore Trap burns ore to earn Critter Fur and Latches for Box Trap upgrades. Both machines work while you are away.

Adding fuel and collecting#

Open a machine, select eligible fuel in your Bag, choose an amount and add it. Each machine holds 100,000 pieces and burns 10,000 pieces per day (416.667 per hour). A full load lasts 10 days. Coal and Tree Meat are excluded.

Mixed fuel burns in proportion to the quantities loaded. Fuel quality changes rewards per piece, not the piece burn rate. Both machines can run at once. Closing the panel or logging out does not pause them; production stops when their fuel runs out.

Use Collect at each machine to move its ready Fur and Latches into your Bag. Rewards remain ready until collected. Collecting keeps leftover Fur points and your Latch progress.

Critter Fur#

Every 300 Fur points earns one Critter Fur. The fuel tables show the contribution of one burned piece. Fractional Fur contributions add up within each machine, so a fuel worth less than one Fur per piece still produces whole Fur over time. These Fur rewards are deterministic.

Latch progress, random rewards and pity#

Both machines contribute to one shared Latch progress meter. They work through missing Latches above your currently owned Box Trap, in tier order. A Latch already waiting in either machine or held in your inventory is skipped. You can earn several different Latches before collecting or buying the next trap.

For each target, the game chooses a hidden random progress threshold. Burning fuel adds progress until that threshold is reached, then awards that Latch. The table's Average progress is the mean threshold; Guaranteed by is the hard pity cap. These progress units are separate from the coins paid for the upgrade.

There is no fixed independent drop roll for each log or ore. Before the guarantee, chance rises along an exponential curve: for progress x and guarantee G, the chance earned is approximately 1 − exp(−1.59362426 × x / G). At half of the guaranteed progress, the chance of having earned the Latch is 54.924%. Any remaining unlucky outcomes award the Latch at the guarantee. This accounts for 20.319% of fresh targets.

Use Early Latch progress for targets below Hunting level 600. For Storm and later Latches (level 600+), use Storm+ Latch progress. Lower-level fuels gain extra progress for those later targets; their Fur reward stays the same.

Awarding a Latch spends its actual hidden threshold and carries excess progress to the next missing Latch. If no higher Latches remain, fuel still earns Fur but no further Latch progress is banked. Collecting or reopening the machine does not reroll the target.

Latch targets and trap upgrades#

Take the matching Latch and coins to Trappy. Buy Box Trap upgrades in order and meet the listed Hunting level. The speed multiplier belongs to your owned trap tier.
