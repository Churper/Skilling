Lure Fire & Ore Trap

Article

The Lure Fire burns logs and the Ore Trap burns ore. Both give Critter Fur and Latches for Box Trap upgrades, even while you're offline.

Lure Fire & Ore Trap calculator: enter how many logs or ore you have.

How it works#

Add logs or ore from your Bag. Each machine holds 100,000 and burns 10,000 a day, so a full load lasts 10 days. Coal and Tree Meat don't work.

You can mix fuels, and both machines can run at once. Press Collect to take your Fur and Latches.

Critter Fur#

The tables below show how much Fur each log or ore gives. Fractions add up, so cheap fuel still makes whole Fur. Fur is not random.

Latches and pity#

Both machines fill the same Latch progress. You get Latches in order, starting after your current trap. Each Latch drops at a random point, and always by its pity. Leftover progress goes to your next Latch.

Storm Latch and up (Lvl 600+) get more progress from each log or ore. Fur stays the same.
