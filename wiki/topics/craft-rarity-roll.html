Craft Rarity Roll

Article

Crafted equipment rolls a rarity. Rarity, SlimePower and potential lines are separate ways to improve the same item.

Refine an existing item#

Use the Blacksmith to raise an eligible item by one rarity step. Ordinary refining stops at Legendary. Open the item's article for its exact recipe and refine materials.

Rings made with a Ring Mould use a different material curve; the item's own refine table has the correct cost. For Legendary to Mythic, use Dupey.

Other equipment upgrades#

Use SlimePower for enhancement and Washing Machine for potential lines. Compare actual item stats and costs before replacing your current equipment.
