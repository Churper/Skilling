Money-making

Article

Make coins by salvaging, selling valuable Frenzy rewards, growing crops and cooking ingredients into more valuable food. Choose a method that fits your levels and how often you want to interact.

Salvaging#

At the Port, open the boat shop's Add-ons and buy the Salvaging Net with 50 × Copper Ore + 100 × Oak Logs + 200 × String. Launch a boat, stop on the ocean and lower the Salvaging Hook. The starter net has no Explorer level requirement.

Salvaging has a 6-hour AFK timer. Real input refills the timer while salvaging is active. After it expires, lower the hook again to restart. Background tabs count toward the same timer. If you close the game with the hook down, returning collects elapsed finds only up to that saved deadline. Net and hook upgrades shorten the interval. They do not improve the chance of each find.

Lost Purse appears on 1.67% of loot rolls. Opening it pays Gold scaled by your Explorer level at opening, 0–8 SlimeCoins and a separate 10% chance of 3–9 Ancient Coin. Gold and SlimeCoins are different currencies.

Selling Frenzy drops, including Meteors#

Gather at an eligible Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing or Hunting spot and claim its Frenzy prompt. The reward tier follows the relevant skill level. A normal claimed reward gives 2–7 of its tier item. A Meteor Frenzy gives Meteor Core even at higher Mining levels, plus Meteor Shards. Its Core reward is included below.

Frenzy items also fuel the Washing Machine. Selling them earns coins but gives up those charges. A Frenzy Bag stores Frenzy items; it does not increase their reward chance. Charging Mystic's Orb replaces the main Frenzy item reward, so turn that off when farming the saleable tier reward.

Luck and Frenzy Surge#

Most eligible spots, including Meteors, check every 10 seconds with 8% base chance. Meat Trees use 7% instead. An unclaimed active prompt blocks a new one. Claiming promptly matters.

Worn Luck gear increases this chance. Total Luck multiplies the base chance by 1 + Luck% / 100. Lucky Duck and Luck-granting Wisps can also contribute. The effect sometimes called a "Frenzy Scroll" is Frenzy Surge: feed Wizard Scroll to the Wizard companion, then activate its perk. It multiplies Frenzy chance by 2.5 for 10 minutes. It increases frequency, not the number of tier items in a claim.

Farming returns#

Plant at a Crop Patch, let the crop grow, then return to harvest and replant. Growth can finish while you are away. A ready crop waits for you; an unattended patch does not stack repeated harvests.

Set your Farming level and bonuses to see each unlocked crop's yield and sale value. Coins after seeds does not deduct compost.

Keep the useful rewards#

Frenzy finds also provide washer charges, Pearls upgrade storage, and crops supply cooking and compost. The Lure Fire and Ore Trap burn logs and ore for Critter Fur, which sells for 100 coins each.
