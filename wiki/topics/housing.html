Player housing

Article

Player housing lets you own a home in the Ville, decorate it and add facilities that you and your visitors can use. Homes, upgrades and ordinary furniture are paid for with Cozy Coupon.

Buying a home#

  1. Buy Cozy Coupon in the SlimeCoin Shop: 10 Slimecoins per coupon.
  2. Carry your coupons in your Bag, then enter the Ville from its mainland entrance.
  3. Open Plots, browse neighborhoods and select an unowned plot. Sign in and confirm the purchase shown in its panel.
  4. Open your home's door to go inside. The My home button in Plots helps you find it again.

You can own one home. Plot location is cosmetic; it does not change the purchase price. Banked coupons must be withdrawn before buying.

Decorating your home#

Inside your home, choose Decorate to browse furniture and Ideas, place and rotate objects, and change your room. Use the home controls to adjust its available styles and exterior colors. Choose Done when you are ready to use the room.

Furniture prices are shown in Cozy Coupons. Check whether an object says Usable by everyone or Decoration for now: a decorative object's appearance can represent a feature that has not been added yet.
