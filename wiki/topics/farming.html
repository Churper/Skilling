Farming

Article

Plant seeds on farm patches • Wait for crops to grow • Harvest for XP & food

🌾🥕
🍅🌽

Click a farm patch to open the crop picker. Select a seed (costs coins), then wait for it to grow. Click again to harvest.

Harvested crops are food - eat them for HP or sell at the store. Higher crops heal more and give more XP.
