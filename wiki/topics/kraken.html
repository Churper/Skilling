Kraken encounter

Article

An ocean event fought from your boat

  1. Summon at the Sacrificial Pool on the mainland. The offering needs 3 Ocean Bait, 2 Pearls, 6 accepted gems and 30 frenzy points. The pool shows accepted items and what you have. Frenzy drops use their washer-charge values; Kraken fish count triple here.
  2. Follow the event alert and sail out. Prepare a boat, food and combat supplies before the Kraken surfaces. This is a summoned ocean event with a shared timer, separate from Summon Bell bosses.
  3. Break all four tentacles, then attack the exposed head. Your tentacle and head progress is personal: other players cannot complete your fight for you. Keep landing attacks while steering out of marked attacks and ink hazards.
  4. Finish the head before the event ends. Rewards are awarded for your completed kill; being nearby or only damaging a tentacle does not earn completion loot. Finishing your fight leaves the event available to other players until its timer ends.
Bring 10+ food, buff potions, and your best gear. Bosses can be fought in a party!
💡 Luck stat from Washing Machine increases equipment and potion drop chances!
