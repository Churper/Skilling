Cave Fishing

Dedicated PvP cave spots • levels 600–2000

These fish come only from their dedicated PvP cave fishing spots. Meet the listed Fishing level and find the matching spot; they never appear at ordinary or mixed ocean spots. Catches are raw and can be cooked. The cycle times below are base times, before gathering bonuses.
