Kit

Article

Your Kit holds the cosmetics that change your slime's look, your Name Badge collection and your Toolbelt. Combat equipment is managed separately.

Cosmetic slots#

The cosmetic slots are Face, Hat, Effect, Badge, Weapon. Each accepts its matching cosmetic. For example, Cave Lure is a Hat cosmetic, rather than a fishing tool.

Equip cosmetics from your Bag. Only one cosmetic occupies each slot at a time. Name Badges have their own collection: add the badge from your Bag, then choose it inside Kit. Cosmetics do not add combat or gathering stats.

See Name Badge rewards and styles, Tools and the Toolbelt, and all cosmetics.
