Frenzies

Article
On this pageClaiming a frenzyReward tiersImproving your chance

A frenzy is a timed bonus on the node you are gathering. Click the highlighted node to claim it before its timer ends.

Claiming a frenzy#

  1. Keep gathering and watch for the highlighted node.
  2. Click the node while the offer is active. The bonus is separate from your regular gathering output.
  3. For automatic claims, enable Auto Frenzy and keep Frenzy Charm in your Bag or Bank. An automatic claim spends one charm; manual claims do not.

Frenzy Bag stores eligible frenzy rewards. If a Mystic Orb is set to charge, the frenzy charges it instead of giving the tier reward. Base resource bonuses and XP still apply.

Meteor frenzies give Meteor Core instead of the normal Mining tier reward. Hunting uses 1.5 catches of base XP; its XP rule differs from the other gathering skills.

Improving your chance#

Luck multiplies the frenzy roll chance. Wizard Scroll powers the companion Frenzy Surge perk. See Pet Garden for its charges and conditions, and Washing Machine for Luck potential lines.
