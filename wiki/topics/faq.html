FAQ

Article

Answers to the questions players actually ask

FrenzyWhat is a frenzy, and what are drops like Enchanted Twigs or Gold Nuggets for?
A frenzy is a short bonus window while mining, woodcutting, or fishing. When you hear the alert and the node changes color or gets a special effect, click it before the window ends for bonus XP and a frenzy drop. Those drops, like Enchanted Twigs, Gold Pebbles, Gold Nuggets, and Minnows, are mainly fuel for the Washing Machine: deposit them there for charges used to roll gear potentials. Higher-tier frenzy drops give more charges. Luck increases how often frenzies happen.
MoneyHow do I make early money?
Level up whatever you enjoy. Nearly everything makes more money as the levels climb: cooked food, farming, monster drops, gathered materials, boss loot, and items other players want. Check the Player Exchange to see what people are buying.
TutorialHow do I reopen the tutorial?
The tutorial auto-opens for new players. If you closed it, open the skill panel, then use Quests at the top.
Special DropsWhat do Magic Seeds, Bird Nests, Eggplants, Golden Fish, and Uncut Gems do?

ClanDo clan points only come from tasks?
No. Clans have two point tracks. Your member points come from your own XP gains: 1 clan point per 1,000 total XP gained after your previous XP snapshot. The clan's total points and level rise from member XP gains plus completed weekly clan tasks. Weekly task XP is clan-only, so it raises the clan but does not add to your member points. The Activity tab shows task item turn-ins directly. New XP rows show the top skill XP gains that caused the clan points; older XP rows fall back to total XP gains because no reason was stored on those rows.
CombatWhere should I train combat early?
Go south to the Slime Crab cave. If the crab is not outside, enter his cave. Do not confuse it with a dragon den or demon den. You can also press M and use the resource dropdown to locate combat targets, which show with a purple ping. Wolves and Ghosts are auto-aggressive. Bosses are also good training once your items, Slime Power, and Washing Machine potential rolls are strong enough.
DummyWhy did the training dummy stop giving XP?
Training dummies give reduced XP for the combat style you are using only until that style reaches level 20. They do not give Hitpoints XP. After that, train on real enemies, especially Slime Crab early. See Combat for the full combat section.
ArrowsDo I need arrows for ranged?
Arrows are optional currently, but they improve your ranged damage when crafted and equipped.
PrayersDo prayers cost anything?
No prayer cost right now. Protection prayers and damage types are still being tuned, so they may not block every hit everywhere.
WasherHow do I get Washing Machine charges?
Deposit frenzy drops into the washer. Higher-tier frenzy drops give more charges. The washer uses charges, not coins, for potential rolls.
LinesWhat washer lines should I aim for?
Life Steal is comfort for combat. Luck means more frenzies. XP Boost is for skilling gear. Damage, Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Boss Damage, and Tempo depend on what you are fighting.
XP BoostCan XP Boost roll above 1%?
No. XP Boost is exactly 1% and is rarer than most potential lines. The maximum full XP set is 9 worn slots with 3 XP lines each.
WeaponsDo washing lines on all weapons apply?
Potential lines on worn gear apply to you regardless of combat style. A weapon's raw damage stats only matter when you are using that style.
CraftingCan I craft with base ore and logs?
The first normal equipment recipes use Tin Ore and Birch Logs. Plain ore and logs are starter materials, but they are not the main first gear path.
PetsHow do pets gather for me?
Assign a job in the Pet Garden and put Tree Meat in the shared trough. Each meat fuels one working slimelet for 24 minutes. Assigned companions can work too; being unequipped is not a requirement.
PerksHow do I use pet perks?
Feed boss scrolls from your Bag to any owned slimelet to add to the shared charge pool. Equip a companion to use the perks. Pet Garden lists each scroll, charge amount, effect and condition.
BankHow do I bank from a node?
Frost Scroll adds Pocket Bank charges. With an active companion and charges, Pocket Bank deposits stackable items remotely. Its default key is B; keybinds can be changed. See Pet Garden.
FaithWhat is the Sacred Grove or crop circle?
It is the Faith event. Channel it to full, then collect the orbs for even more XP. See Crop Circles (Faith).
SkinsWhat do paintbrushes do?
Use Paint in the Pet Garden to apply a boss skin to a slimelet. Painting consumes one matching brush; replacing the skin does not refund it. Browse paintbrush previews and sources.
