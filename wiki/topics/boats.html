Article

Boat tiers, sailing stats and material costs

Visit the Port to use your free Tin Skiff and buy boat upgrades. Buy each tier in order; the costs below are for that upgrade alone, not the total from the starter boat. Materials can come from your Bag or Bank.

Each boat upgrade increases sailing speed and boost capacity. You can select any owned boat's appearance while keeping the speed and boost capacity of your highest unlocked tier.
