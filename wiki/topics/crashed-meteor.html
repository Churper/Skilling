Crashed Meteor

Article
On this pageMining rewardsFinding and using a meteor

Crashed Meteors are shared, depleting mining nodes. Use the minimap meteor marker to find the current event.

Mining rewards#

Meteor variants
Mainland meteor6001 × Meteor Shard
Island meteor1,5002 × Meteor Shard

No entries match. Try another name or clear the filter.

These are base quantities before gathering bonuses. Ordinary mining gives Meteor Shard; Meteor Core comes from claiming a meteor frenzy. Mining a meteor does not grant a Core on every hit.

Finding and using a meteor#

  1. Find the meteor marker on the in-game minimap.
  2. Equip a pickaxe and mine while durability remains.
  3. Click any mining frenzy before its timer expires.

Other players can deplete the same node. The mainland meteor moves after depletion, so a fixed wiki map position is not a live meteor tracker. See AFK gathering for gathering limits and Meteor Shard for crafting uses.
