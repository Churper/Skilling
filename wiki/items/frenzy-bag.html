Frenzy Bag stores Frenzy rewards separately from your Bag. It starts with space for 100 items, and permanent upgrades increase its capacity.

On this pageOverviewStorage & upgradesSources

Storage & upgrades#

Frenzy rewards

Stores: enchanted wood, gold pieces, Meteor Cores and frenzy fish.

Buy: 1,000,000 coins from Mystic Slime.

Capacity: 100 items, up to 1,000. Each Mystic Slime chore permanently adds 100 spaces. Complete them in any order.

Right-click the bag to view your Frenzy Charms or turn Auto Frenzy on or off.
