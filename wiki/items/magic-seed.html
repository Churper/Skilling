Uses#

Magic Seeds power the Magic Blossom in the Pet Garden and let you change your active companion away from the Garden.

Magic Seed uses
Plant in the Magic Patch1 seed per planting+10% pet gathering XP for 10 hours per seed. Additional seeds extend the blossom timer from its current end, or from now if it has expired.
Click a Magic Seed in your bag, then select another owned slimelet1 seed per changeEquips the chosen companion. Selecting the active companion makes no change; dismissing a companion is free.
