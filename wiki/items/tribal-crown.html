On this pageOverviewKit and appearanceSources

Kit and appearance#

This is a cosmetic for the Hat slot. Open your Kit to manage cosmetics. It changes appearance without adding combat or gathering stats.

Equip it from your Bag for that cosmetic slot. Equipping another cosmetic in the same slot replaces the current one; removing it returns it to your Bag.
