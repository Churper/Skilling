How the selection works

80% of selections choose uniformly from the 5 most recently unlocked resources (or all unlocked resources if fewer). The remaining 20% choose uniformly from every unlocked resource. The table combines both chances for each resource.

If you have N unlocked resources and R in the recent group, each recent resource has 80% / R + 20% / N chance. Older resources have 20% / N chance.

For resources below level 600, each quantity from 2 to 5 is equally likely. Resources requiring level 600 or higher give exactly 1. This depends on the chosen resource, not your selected level.

See ocean salvaging for obtaining bundles and upgrading catch speed.
