Log Basket stores eligible logs separately from your Bag. It starts with space for 100 items, and permanent upgrades increase its capacity.

On this pageOverviewStorage & upgradesSources

Storage & upgrades#

Level 600+ logs

Stores: all level 600+ logs.

Found: 1/100 per successful chop of those trees, or 1/100 per Swamp Frog kill.

Capacity: 100 logs. Buy permanent upgrades from the Woodcutter with Diamonds.
