Lodge fuel#

Load this into the Ore Trap. Each piece burned contributes 1,350 Fur reward points (4.5 Critter Fur), 90 progress toward early Latches, or 3,000 progress when the target Latch requires level 600 or higher. Fractional Fur contributions accumulate until a whole Fur is ready.
