Hunter Name Badge is a cosmetic Name Badge earned at Hunter level 300. Add it to your Kit and select it for the Badge slot to change the appearance of your name. More progress unlocks additional badge styles.

On this pageOverviewKit and appearanceSources

Kit and appearance#

This is a cosmetic for the Badge slot. Open your Kit to manage cosmetics. It changes appearance without adding combat or gathering stats.

Unlock: Hunter level 300. Reach the required Hunter level. The badge is an achievement reward, separate from a mastery cape.

Click the badge in your Bag to add it to the Kit's badge collection. Select it there to equip it; select the active badge again to remove it. It stays in the badge collection when removed. Right-click for its available options and unlocked styles.
