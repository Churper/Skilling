Opening a Lost Purse#

Open the purse from your Bag. One purse is consumed for the rewards below. Each purse rolls Gold, SlimeCoins and the Ancient Coin bonus separately. Your Explorer level when opening determines its Gold range.

Rewards per Lost Purse
GoldAlways15,000 + 150 × Explorer level, up to 3 times that amountEvery whole-number amount in the inclusive range is equally likely.
SlimeCoins (at least one)88.889%0–8Every integer amount has a 11.111% chance, including zero.
Ancient Coin1 / 103–9Independent bonus. Each quantity has a 1.429% overall chance (14.286% when the bonus occurs).

XP and finding the purse#

Opening gives 0 XP. The regular salvaging haul that finds it awards Explorer XP separately, based on your Explorer level and progress. It gives no Fishing, Mining or Woodcutting XP for a purse. A worn Leviathan Wisp's extra loot roll gives no additional XP.
