Box Trap upgrade#

Give Trappy 1 × Ghost Latch and 2,000,000,000 coins for the Ghost Box Trap. Needs Hunting 2,250 and the trap before it.

How to get it#

Burn logs or ore at the Lure Fire or Ore Trap. It takes about 10,000,000,000 progress and never more than 20,000,000,000. See Latches.
