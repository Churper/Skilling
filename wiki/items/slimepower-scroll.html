SlimePower upgrade costs#

Choose your equipment and current and target SlimePower. Coin costs include retrying after failed rolls and lost levels. Scrolls give guaranteed levels up to SP15; SP16–20 use coin rolls.

SP15: +113 ATK, +58 DEF

Minimum coins: 15,674. Average coins: 3,753,279.09. Maximum coins: No fixed maximum below SP15.

Scrolls used: 0. Ancient Coin needed if buying those scrolls: 0.

ATK and DEF above are the added SlimePower stats. Minimum assumes every coin roll succeeds. Average includes recovery from downgrades. Saved SP15+ failures reduce the attempts left until its guarantee.
