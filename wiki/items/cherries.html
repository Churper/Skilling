Cherries is food that restores 244 HP before healing bonuses. It is grown through Farming at level 300.

On this pageOverviewHarvest yieldSourcesUsed in recipes

Harvest yield#

Yield depends on your Farming level when you harvest, the patch's compost and your equipped Wisps' extra-crop bonus. The source table lists the base yield at the unlock level.

Use the combined extra-crop percentage from your equipped Rabbit Wisp. Luck, potions and mastery capes do not multiply the crop count.

Every 10 Farming levels above 300 adds one crop before compost. The random base yield improves as you level; at 350, it always reaches the base maximum of 10. The level bonus continues above that point. Compost applies next, followed by the extra-crop bonus. Fractional extra crops are awarded by a separate chance.

The calculator assumes enough free Bag space. A partially full Bag can reduce the delivered quantity. See Crop Patch for planting, growing and harvesting.
