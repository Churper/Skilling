A coin-charged melee ring. Right-click for Charge or Setting. Charges pay for an optional damage boost on landed melee hits.

Charging and settings#

Equip the Bee Ring, right-click it and choose Charge to add coins. One coin becomes one charge. Choose Setting to pick how many charges a boosted melee hit uses. The default is Green: +10% melee damage for 40 charges per hit.

The boost applies to landed melee hits against monsters. A miss does not spend charges. You need at least the setting's full charge cost to use the boost; with too few charges, you keep making ordinary melee attacks. Settings affect the damage bonus and charge cost, rather than attack speed.

Saving charges#

Reclaim Potion has a 50% chance to save the whole charge cost of a boosted hit. An equipped Melee Cape saves 50%; an equipped Melee Cape (t) saves 60%. Saved charges keep the damage bonus.

The cape and potion roll independently. With Reclaim Potion, the untrimmed cape saves 75% of charge costs on average; the trimmed cape saves 80%. The cape must be equipped. A save roll still requires enough charges for the selected setting.
