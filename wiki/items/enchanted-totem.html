Enchanted Totem is a frenzy item that gives 5 washer charges when deposited at the Washing Machine. Depositing consumes the item. Charges are used to reroll equipment potential.

On this pageOverviewEffects & useSources

Effects & use#

Deposit this item from your bag, bank or Frenzy Bag at the Washing Machine. One item supplies 5 charges. See all frenzy items to compare charge values.
