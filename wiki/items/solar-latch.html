A lodge reward used with 200,000 coins to upgrade your Box Trap to Solar at Trappy. Requires Hunting level 250 and the preceding trap tier. Earn it by burning eligible fuel at the Lure Fire or Ore Trap, then collect it from the machine.

On this pageOverviewBox Trap upgradeEarning this LatchSources

Box Trap upgrade#

Give Trappy 1 × Solar Latch and 200,000 coins to buy the Solar Box Trap. You need Hunting level 250 and the previous owned trap tier. The upgraded trap has a 2.1× speed multiplier.

Earning this Latch#

The Lure Fire and Ore Trap work toward missing Latches in order above your currently owned trap. This target's average threshold is 1,000,000 progress and its guarantee is 2,000,000. Use the early Latch progress column for each fuel. Collect the Latch from whichever machine shows it ready. Latches already held or waiting in a machine are skipped.
