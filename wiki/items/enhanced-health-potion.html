Combine in your Bag#

Drag Bird Nest onto Health Potion in your Bag. This uses one of each to make one Enhanced Health Potion. The direction matters: drag the Bird Nest onto the Health Potion. With stacks, it combines matching pairs together, up to the available inputs and available Bag space.
