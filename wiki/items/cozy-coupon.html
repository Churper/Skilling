Housing currencies

Cozy Coupons buy your home, home upgrades and furniture in the Ville. Buy one for 10 Slimecoins in the SlimeCoin Shop. Keep coupons in your Bag when purchasing; banked coupons are not spent. Your first home costs 10 coupons.

On this pageOverviewSpending Cozy CouponsSources

Spending Cozy Coupons#

Use coupons in the Ville to buy a home, its next upgrade or furniture. Open Plots to choose a home; go inside your house and choose Decorate to browse furniture. The purchase panel shows the coupon price before you buy.

Purchases use coupons from your Bag. Withdraw banked coupons first. You must sign in to buy a home, and you can own one home. See home prices and upgrades.
