Coins

🪙

Currency

Type
Account balance
Bag space
None

Coins are an account currency balance shown in the game interface. They do not occupy bag slots.

Getting Coins#

Sell items at the General Store, collect coin rewards and trade items through the Player Exchange.

Spending Coins#

Spend Coins on supplies, tool upgrades, planting costs and other services. Player trading also uses Coins.

See Currencies for other balances and currency items.
