Box Trap

Article

The Box Trap is your Hunting tool. Its owned tier sets gathering speed and is shown in your Toolbelt.

Upgrade with Trappy in tier order. The requirement below refers to Hunting level, and each price buys that upgrade in addition to the previous tiers.
