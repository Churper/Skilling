Fishing Pole

Article

The Fishing Pole is your Fishing tool. Its owned tier sets gathering speed and is shown in your Toolbelt.

Upgrade at the Blacksmith in tier order. The requirement below refers to Fishing level, and each price buys that upgrade in addition to the previous tiers.
