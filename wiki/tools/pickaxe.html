Pickaxe

Article

The Pickaxe is your Mining tool. Its owned tier sets gathering speed and is shown in your Toolbelt.

Upgrade at the Blacksmith in tier order. The requirement below refers to Mining level, and each price buys that upgrade in addition to the previous tiers.
