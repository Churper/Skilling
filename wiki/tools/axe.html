Axe

Article

The Axe is your Woodcutting tool. Its owned tier sets gathering speed and is shown in your Toolbelt.

Upgrade at the Blacksmith in tier order. The requirement below refers to Woodcutting level, and each price buys that upgrade in addition to the previous tiers.
