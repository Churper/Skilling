SlimeFoot has a 1 in 4 chance to appear on an island after players finish Slimeshroom. The first landed hit starts a five-minute timer.

During the fight#

Hover over SlimeFoot for the time left. Spawn alerts are under Settings › Desktop Notifications › Notify For.

Each landed hit gives 3× Slimeshroom combat EXP and 75% of that as Hitpoints EXP. Each also has a 5% chance for 1 SlimeCoin, multiplied by SC Mastery. A landed second hit from Lion's Fork counts. You keep these earnings if you leave early.

Finish rewards#

Land at least one hit and be within 120 units of SlimeFoot at the finish. Your loot goes straight to your Bank. Luck and Fortune do not change these rolls.
