Loch Ness Slime is a cooperative skilling boss on Loch Slime Isle. Gather Thorn ammunition, use five shore launchers to bring him out of the water, then gather from his exposed head. Survival, Fishing, Woodcutting, Mining and Hunting all contribute.

Access and island#

Loch Slime Isle is part of the ordinary world. Sail to the island and land at its south dock. Activate the travel statue near the dock to unlock quick travel for later visits. A boat and reward pile are nearby. The bank is on the right hill; the Wisperator is on the left hill. The worn dirt patch on the northern shore marks where the head lands.

Skills and ammunition#

Choose a gathering method and equip its tool. Every method starts at skill level 1. Each successful gather gives exactly one unstackable Thorn item. Gather yield bonuses do not increase this, and Thorn gathering never starts a Frenzy. Thorn ammunition cannot be banked and is cleared, together with loaded shots, when you leave or the boss dies.

Equipped tool speed shortens gathering. Survival uses a virtual tool speed based on your Survival level instead of a held tool. Success chance also affects throughput. The active fishing spot moves, while depleted bushes, trees, rocks and hedgehog spawns recover over time. Three shared hedgehog spawns can limit a group that all chooses Hunting.

Encounter cycle#

  1. Gather and load. While Loch is in the water, collect ammunition and click its matching launcher to load and fire. Each accepted shot removes 20 blue exposure HP.
  2. Make him surface. Solo exposure is 200 blue HP (10 shots). Each additional contributor adds 2 shots, up to 5 contributors. That gives 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 shots for one through five contributors.
  3. Gather the head. After 10 seconds of surfacing, the head rests on shore for 22 seconds. Use one of the same five skills directly on the head. Every successful head gather removes 4 red HP.
  4. Repeat. The shared red health starts at 160 HP and persists between exposures. If health remains, Loch returns to the water and the blue bar resets. Defeat him by completing 40 successful head gathers across the group.

The standard shared boss bar shows the encounter health. Stronger tools improve gathering speed; they do not increase the damage of each shot or head gather.

Hazards#

Watch for tail attacks and dangerous thorns around gathering nodes. Warnings last 3 seconds; a hit deals 15% of your maximum HP. Shared attacks are spaced at least 45 seconds apart. Move away from warnings and active thorn patches before resuming gathering.

Contribution and XP#

Rewards are personal. Each accepted launcher shot contributes 20 points and each accepted head gather contributes 4. Half is credited immediately; the other half waits until the boss dies. Every 100 credited points grants one reward-pile search, and leftover progress carries over. Use the reward pile near the south dock to search once or search all available credits.

Completing the fight also adds 15% of your full personal contribution for each additional qualifying contributor, up to five contributors. To qualify, a player must contribute at least 20 points or 5% of the round’s total contribution, whichever is greater. Spectators and NPCs do not count. The reserved half and team bonus are added together, then rounded down once when completion rewards are credited. Leaving the boss unfinished does not pay that reserved completion credit.

Gathering, loading, firing and head gathering also award skill XP. Some XP is reserved until the boss dies; completion XP follows the skills used for accepted shots. Meaningful party participation increases this reserved completion XP. It does not multiply the XP already earned from gathering, loading, firing or head gathering. Spectators do not receive completion XP. Leaving keeps XP already earned but clears unused ammunition.

Read the party column using all qualifying contributors. For Hunting, count Hunters whose accepted launcher shots contribute at least 20 points and at least 5% of the round’s total contribution each, then use the larger of the party and Hunter multipliers. This extra Hunting support accounts for sharing the three hedgehog spawns. These multipliers affect reserved kill XP only, not total XP or reward-pile contribution.

In the balance model, completed solo Survival fights target about 1.25 times the XP rate of comparable ordinary bush gathering. Actual XP rates depend on success chance, travel and completing the fight.

Rewards#

Each search gives exactly one weighted main reward, then rolls every bonus independently. Several bonuses can arrive with the same main reward. Luck, skill level, XP potions and group size do not change these odds per search. Your contribution and completion bonus determine how many searches you earn.
