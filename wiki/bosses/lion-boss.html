Fight Lion solo or with a party from the Task Board. 15,000,000 HP.

Fight#

Attack the statues to charge them. Lion lands when both are at 100%. Each takes 1,000,000 damage to charge. Melee hits give 3× charge. Charge drains if you stop attacking.

Leave the dusty half of the arena within 2 seconds. Protect from Melee blocks Lion’s swipes. Face away when he roars.

Gear pieces are equally likely. A Fortune Potion has a 50% chance to double your Lion Mane and Solar Glass quantities. Each clear also rolls 85% for 1 SlimeCoin before Luck and SC Mastery, up to 30 SC per player from this boss per 24 hours.

Party loot and Luck#

Each shared drop goes to one player. Deal twice as much boss damage for twice the chance to win. Shared rolls use the host’s Luck. Statue damage earns no share. Personal rolls use your own Luck. At +400% Luck, full rolls are 5× as likely and quarter rolls are 2× as likely.

Stay alive in the arena for personal rolls, even if you dealt no damage. Ironslimes need 30% of total damage in a duo, 25% with three players, 20% with four, or 15% with five or six.
