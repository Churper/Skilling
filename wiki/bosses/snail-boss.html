Snail Boss is a boss encounter in Slimeville. Its standard loot includes Snail Shell.

Access#

Open the Task Board and pick the Snail Boss card. In the arena, click a rock in front of the snails to pick a mode: the green rock is Solo, red is Duo and ice is Trio. Attack a snail to start. You get loot and kill credit when every snail in the fight is beaten.

Mechanics and strategy#

Snail Boss: attack patterns
Goo lob (green snail)The green snail lobs goo at every player. It lands where you stood 1.8 s later, hits for 7% of your max HP and leaves a goo puddle.Keep moving so it lands behind you.
Magic and Range shots (green snail)A shot charges over the green snail for 1 s, then flies for 2 s. Unblocked it hits for 35% of your max HP. One shot per set from 70% HP, two from 25% HP.Pray to block it: Protect Magic for Magic, Protect Ranged for Range.
Fire line (red snail)In Duo and Trio, the red snail aims a fire line at every player. It shows for 1.8 s, then races out. It hits for 12% of your max HP and sets you on fire for 4 s.Step sideways out of the line.
Frost wave (ice snail)In Trio, the ice snail sends a frost wave at every player. Each player gets their own safe lane, off to one side of where they stand. A hit does 14% of your max HP and freezes you for 3 s.Move into your own safe lane before the wave reaches you.
Goo puddlesLob puddles later burst into goo balls (11% of your max HP each, and they slow you). Standing in goo hurts more the longer you stay. 1.25 s on clean ground clears it.Stay off the goo and dodge the balls.
ShellAt 50% of its own HP, each snail hides in its shell for 8 s and takes only 10% damage.Hit another snail or break eggs while it hides.

Eggs#

When the snails' total HP drops to 70%, and again at 40%, the snails lay 2 eggs in Solo, 3 eggs in Duo and 4 eggs in Trio, plus 1 more for each extra player in the fight. Anyone can break any egg: it breaks on one click, from anywhere, with any weapon.

  • Break every egg in a wave for 2x damage for 5 s.
  • An egg you leave blasts when its timer runs out (12 s in Solo, 12 s in Duo and 13 s in Trio, 1 second more for each extra egg). It hits players near it for 64% of their max HP, throws goo balls and leaves goo for 2 minutes.

Fight again#

Ring the Summon Bell in the arena to start a new fight.

Party loot#

Stay alive in the boss arena until the fight ends to receive loot. Ironslimes must also meet the minimum damage share.

Personal: each eligible player rolls separately using their own Luck. Regular accounts have no minimum damage requirement.

Shared: the party rolls once using the fight host’s Luck, then one eligible player wins, weighted by damage dealt. You must deal damage to qualify. The listed rate is the party’s chance to get the drop.

For example, 25% of eligible players’ damage gives a 25% chance to win a shared drop. With a 10% drop rate, your overall chance is 2.5% before Luck.

The Ironslime minimum compares your damage with all player damage across the fight, including boss phases and healing. Solo Ironslimes have no minimum. Shared-drop weights count only eligible players: downed players, Ironslimes below their minimum and existing owners of rewards limited to one copy are excluded. Luck changes the drop roll, not your damage weight.

Snail encounters award one set of drop rolls per snail after the whole fight is completed (1, 2, 3 snails). Each shared rare rolls once per snail, then uses the whole encounter’s damage shares to choose a recipient.

Drop details#

  • Snail Shell always drops, 1 per snail.
  • Shared by damage share: Snail Cape, Ring Mould, Shell Plate and Reclaim Potion. The party rolls once, then one player wins, weighted by the damage they did.
  • Personal: every other drop. Each player rolls their own.
  • 25% Luck: Snail Cape, Ring Mould and Shell Plate.
  • Full Luck: the rest.
  • SlimeCoins: 18% per kill, with its own cap of 30 a day.
