Snail Boss is a boss encounter in Slimeville. Its standard loot includes Snail Shell.

Access#

Coming soon. This encounter is not yet open to public entry. Its listed rewards are planned content.

Solo, Duo and Trio encounters contain one, two or three snails. Complete the whole encounter to receive loot and kill credit.

Party loot#

Stay alive in the boss arena until the fight ends to receive loot. Ironslimes must also meet the minimum damage share.

Personal: each eligible player rolls separately using their own Luck. Regular accounts have no minimum damage requirement.

Shared: the party rolls once using the fight host’s Luck, then one eligible player wins, weighted by damage dealt. You must deal damage to qualify. The listed rate is the party’s chance to get the drop.

For example, 25% of eligible players’ damage gives a 25% chance to win a shared drop. With a 10% drop rate, your overall chance is 2.5% before Luck.

The Ironslime minimum compares your damage with all player damage across the fight, including boss phases and healing. Solo Ironslimes have no minimum. Shared-drop weights count only eligible players: downed players, Ironslimes below their minimum and existing owners of rewards limited to one copy are excluded. Luck changes the drop roll, not your damage weight.

Planned Snail encounters award one set of drop rolls per snail after the whole fight is completed (1, 2, 3 snails). Each shared rare rolls once per snail, then uses the whole encounter’s damage shares to choose a recipient.
