Frost Yeti is a boss encounter in Slimeville. Its standard loot includes Frozen Heart.

On this pageAccessFight mechanicsParty lootDrop tablesStandard lootPaintbrushesPotionsEquipment

Access#

Open the Task Board and choose the boss fight.

Mechanics and strategy#

Frost Yeti: attack patterns
Frozen openingDeposit 20 logs into the arena braziers to free the Yeti.Bring logs for the thaw before entering.
Ice breathThe frontal breath damages and freezes players caught in its cone, setting up a follow-up slam.Move out of the breath cone before it freezes you.
Enrage and iciclesAt 25% HP it enrages. Falling icicles show a ground warning for 1.5 seconds.Leave the marked spots before impact; an icicle hit freezes you.

Party loot#

Stay alive in the boss arena until the fight ends to receive loot. Ironslimes must also meet the minimum damage share.

Every eligible player rolls separately using their own Luck. Table chances are per player; damage share does not change them. Regular accounts have no minimum damage requirement.

The Ironslime minimum compares your damage with all player damage across the fight, including boss phases and healing. Solo Ironslimes have no minimum. Shared-drop weights count only eligible players: downed players, Ironslimes below their minimum and existing owners of rewards limited to one copy are excluded. Luck changes the drop roll, not your damage weight.
