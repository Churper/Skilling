Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

SlimeVille does not track you, serve ads, or collect analytics. We never store your IP address, only a salted, irreversible hash used to enforce bans. See also our Terms of Service.

1. Guest play

If you play without an account, your save is stored only in your browser and is never uploaded. Clearing your browser data erases it.

While you are connected, your display name, appearance, position, and actions are sent to our relay server and broadcast to other players, and anything you type in public chat is handled exactly as described below.

2. Registered accounts

If you create an account, the following is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase:

  • Username, chosen at registration
  • Password, hashed and salted by Supabase Auth. We never see or store your plaintext password.
  • Recovery email, only if you provide one. It is optional.
  • Game save data: your skills, inventory, equipment, coins, and progress
  • Leaderboard stats: your name, skill levels, and boss kill counts

3. Fair play and moderation records

To keep the game fair and safe, we also keep:

  • Anticheat event records: automated notes about unusual gameplay, such as an impossible resource rate, tied to your account. We review these and prune them on a rolling basis.
  • Moderation records: mutes, bans, and their reasons.
  • Player reports: if someone reports you, or you report someone, the report and the relevant chat lines go to a private moderator channel on Discord.
  • A salted hash of your connection address, described below.

4. Your IP address

We never store or log your raw IP address. The multiplayer relay passes it through a keyed one-way hash and keeps only the result, which cannot be reversed back into an address. That hash exists for one reason: so a ban or mute cannot be dodged by making a new account.

Our hosting providers, GitHub Pages, Render, and Supabase, may keep their own short-lived connection logs as part of running their infrastructure. That is outside our control and we do not have access to it.

5. What we do not collect

  • Device or browser fingerprints
  • Advertising trackers, marketing pixels, or product analytics of any kind
  • Your location. We see aggregate traffic counts per country from our CDN, which are not tied to any individual player, and we do not collect or store location data about you.
  • Payment or card details. Donations are handled by Ko-fi and its payment processors, and we never see your card.

We set no cookies of our own. Supabase sets an auth session token when you log in, and our CDN and hosting providers may set their own operational cookies, such as Cloudflare's bot-management cookie. None of these are used to track you across other sites.

6. Third parties

Your data is never sold or rented, and never shared for advertising. These services are used to run the game, and each necessarily sees your IP address when your browser connects to it:

  • Supabase: authentication and database storage
  • Render: hosts the multiplayer relay server
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the game files
  • Cloudflare: sits in front of the site, serving and protecting it, and gives us aggregate traffic counts per country
  • Google Fonts and the jsDelivr and esm.sh CDNs: serve fonts and the 3D engine the game is built on
  • Discord: receives player reports and moderation alerts in a private staff channel, and hosts our community server
  • Ko-fi: handles voluntary donations. If you donate, we receive the name, message, and email you give Ko-fi, and we may match a donation to a game account.
  • ScapeLikes: a game listing site that receives an anonymous online-player count, and, if you choose to vote for SlimeVille there, a check tied to your account identifier

Chat translation runs entirely in your own browser, so your messages are never sent to a translation service.

7. Multiplayer and chat

When connected to multiplayer, your display name, slime appearance, position, and actions are broadcast to other players in real time through our relay server. Those pass through in memory and are not logged or stored.

Chat is different, and we want to be exact about it. Public and clan messages are not written to our database, but the relay keeps a rolling in-memory moderation buffer of recent messages for up to 24 hours so moderators can review reports and tune filters. That buffer is never written to disk and is lost whenever the server restarts. Recent public messages are also briefly replayed to players who join or reconnect, so what you say in public chat may be shown to someone who arrives shortly after.

We do not read or monitor private messages, and they are not placed in that buffer. Automated filters do apply to them, and a private message caught by a filter is recorded as part of the moderation record above.

8. Ads and tracking

There are no ads. There are no tracking scripts, pixels, or analytics tools on this site.

9. Data deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, contact Burga on Discord or open an issue on GitHub. Guest data can be deleted by clearing your browser's local storage.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. Changes are noted by the "last updated" date above.

Any translation of this policy is provided for convenience only. If a translation and this English version differ, the English version governs.
